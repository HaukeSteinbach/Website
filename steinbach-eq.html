EQ Module

8-band parametric EQ with auto-gain compensation.

Drag handles on the frequency graph to shape the tonal balance. Right-click for filter type and slope options. Auto Gain keeps your loudness matched so every change is a fair comparison.

  • 7 filter types: Low Cut, Low Shelf, Bell, Notch, High Shelf, High Cut, Band Pass
  • Up to 48 dB/oct: Butterworth cascade up to 8th order for surgical high and low cut filtering
  • Auto Gain: Log-average gain compensation across 256 frequencies, 10 ms smoothed, keeps A/B comparisons at matched loudness
  • Click-free automation: Per-block coefficient interpolation and 15 ms parameter pre-smoothing eliminate all zipper noise
  • Double precision: TDF-II biquad states in 64-bit for numerical stability at extreme Q values and low frequencies
SAT Module

Multiband saturation that adds character, not mud.

Split the signal into four bands with Linkwitz-Riley crossovers, then saturate each independently. Add warmth to the low-mids without clouding the highs. Drive the high-mids without affecting the bass.

  • Iron waveshaper: Asymmetric transformer-core model, harder on positive half-cycles, generating predominantly even harmonics for a musical analogue character
  • 4-band LR4 crossover: Linkwitz-Riley 4th-order splits at three adjustable crossover frequencies from 20 Hz to 20 kHz
  • Auto output compensation: Normalised to a -22 dBFS reference so drive sounds like saturation, not a volume jump
  • Console stereo simulation: Link off gives L and R independent drive amounts with per-band variation, widening the stereo image through asymmetric harmonic content
TR Module

Transient shaping per frequency band.

A dual-envelope follower separates the attack and sustain phases of the signal in real time. Control them independently, per frequency band, without touching the EQ.

  • Dual IIR envelopes: Fast and slow first-order followers detect transient content. The ratio T = (fast - slow) / (fast + slow) identifies attack vs. sustain
  • Attack and sustain: Independent gain from -24 to +24 dB applied only during the respective phase
  • Speed control: Scales both time constants simultaneously from 0.5 ms (percussive) to 200 ms (ambient), adapting detection to any source
  • Same 4-band split as SAT with independent crossover positions, so TR can work in different frequency ranges simultaneously
