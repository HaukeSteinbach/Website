Formerly Steinbach EQ

Steinbach EQ is now Steinbach Chain. Nothing has been discontinued and nothing has been taken away. The plugin grew into a full channel strip and was renamed for what it had become.

The equaliser, the saturation and the transient shaping you know are still there. They now sit in an order you set yourself, in front of a limiter, with a level matched comparison and a null test to check the result.

If you own Steinbach EQ and are unsure what the change means for your licence, write to the studio and you will get a straight answer.
